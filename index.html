Discovery: I Will — beta privacy notice

Effective September 29, 2026. Contact: cmatt1140@gmail.com.

Your writing and records

I Will stores study answers, drafts, series progress, I Wills, habits, recorded outcomes and reminder preferences in the app's local database on your device. The current beta does not offer accounts, iCloud/CloudKit synchronization, a developer-operated backup service or cross-device recovery. Your iPhone and iPad have separate local records.

The app does not intentionally send this writing to the developer, an AI service or a synchronization provider. Scripture, fonts and reading-plan assets are bundled. The app has no advertising or app-integrated analytics service. Apple's operating system, App Store and TestFlight have their own network and diagnostic behavior described below.

The database is not encrypted by I Will itself. iOS provides device protection; use a device passcode and protect access to your device and Apple Account. Device or computer backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. That is different from I Will synchronization, and the app does not promise that a backup exists or can restore your writing. Do not uninstall or clear app data to troubleshoot.

Saving and deletion

Only writing acknowledged as saved locally or as a saved local draft has been persisted. Closing or terminating the app before acknowledgement can lose newer keystrokes. Device loss, storage failure, deleting the app or clearing its data can lose local records.

Deleting a study removes that study's active answers and study-owned drafts. Independent I Wills, habits and their records are preserved unless you deliberately delete those separately. Deletion is not a guarantee that old data has been forensically removed from database pages, device backups or other operating-system copies. There is no cloud deletion operation because this beta has no app sync service.

Reminders

Local reminders are optional and require notification permission. By default they use generic text. Personal details appear only when you explicitly choose that option, and may then be visible to someone looking at your lock screen or notification history. Changing a reminder or turning it off does not remove a screenshot or notification someone has already seen. Device-specific permission and notification presentation are controlled by iOS.

TestFlight and feedback

For email invitations, the developer supplies your invitation email to Apple and can see invitation/installation status, device and OS information, session and crash counts, and feedback. TestFlight automatically shares crash reports with the developer. If you send a screenshot or comment, its contents and associated device information are shared through Apple's TestFlight system. Email sent to the support address is also received by the developer and the email service.

Use fictional entries for testing and remove private writing from screenshots and comments. Do not send passwords, verification codes, database exports or sensitive personal journals. The developer will not ask to inspect or reset your local records for routine testing.

Our beta support practice is to use feedback only for testing/support, restrict access to the owner, and review/delete owner-held feedback copies within 90 days after the beta closes unless they are needed for an unresolved issue or an applicable obligation. Apple's retention and deletion behavior is governed by Apple, not this app. This retention review is manual, not an automatic deletion system.

Questions

Contact cmatt1140@gmail.com for beta support, invitation removal or questions about feedback held by the developer. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase writing stored solely on your device. No public invitation link is planned.
